#  World Cup Statistics Data Analysis
 
##  Project Overview 
 
This project focuses on performing **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on FIFA World Cup statistics using Python. The objective is to analyze tournament history, team performance, match results, goals, attendance, and player statistics, and extract meaningful insights through data analysis and visualization. 
 
--- 
 
##  Dataset 
 
- **Dataset Name:** FIFA World Cup Statistics Dataset 
- **Format:** CSV 
- **Files Used:** 
  - `WorldCups.csv` 
  - `WorldCupMatches.csv` 
  - `WorldCupPlayers.csv` 
 
--- 
 
##  Project Objectives 
 
- Understand the structure of the World Cup datasets. 
- Clean and preprocess the data. 
- Handle missing values and duplicate records. 
- Analyze World Cup winners and team performance. 
- Analyze goals scored across tournaments. 
- Compare home and away team performance. 
- Analyze World Cup attendance and participation trends. 
- Explore player positions and player statistics. 
- Create visualizations to identify trends and patterns. 
- Generate meaningful insights from the data.

##  Key Insights

-  **Brazil** is the most successful country in FIFA World Cup history based on tournament victories.
-  **Goals scored and average goals per match** vary across different World Cup tournaments.
-  The **number of participating teams and matches increased** over the years.
-  **World Cup attendance** reflects the tournament's growing popularity and global reach.
-  **Home and away team performance** shows noticeable differences in match results and goal-scoring patterns.
